** The substitution containment spin-out (SUB) — a LIVE bug, found and fixed

Resequenced ahead of the POL remainder. ~shift~/~subst~ treated ~expr-champ~ as a
closed leaf, so a lambda whose body was a map literal silently dropped its beta
argument — ~?bvar0 : Nat~ printed at top level with **0 errors**.

- **Ruling (D)**: ~expr-champ~ IS a closed runtime value; the fix makes the
  invariant true by construction rather than policing it.
- **SUB.1** (~f19d6f56~) tripwire at the three ~nf~-persisting boundaries —
  silent-wrong became loud-refuse; a probe closed the cache question (no route
  def-stores the poisoned shape ⇒ no ~.pnet~ invalidation needed).
- **SUB.3a** (~7ea49168~) **the fix**: ~nf~ opens binders NbE-style (deterministic
  ~#%nbe~ fvars), normalizes, and re-abstracts — containers can only capture
  fvars, which ~shift~/~subst~ already treat as identity.
- **SUB.3b** (~036b59f7~) the wider sibling in the narrowing subsystem: a
  catch-all was silently dropping bindings, AND ~narrow-match~ had no map/vec
  decomposition — ~box ?y = {:a 5N}~ returned ~nil~. Both fixed.
- **Hot-scan** (~8ec5e507~) the fast-path scan got explicit arms with the
  reflective walk retained as fallback + differential oracle: **6.9× per walk**.

** PLT_CS_COMPILE_LIMIT adopted — an 18% suite win, found in passing (~6323587e~)
