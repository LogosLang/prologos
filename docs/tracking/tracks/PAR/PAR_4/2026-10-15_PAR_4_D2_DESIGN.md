## 1. Purpose and scope

PAR 4 makes S0, the monotone value stratum, run as fast as the hardware allows, with **correctness as a property of
the architecture rather than of care**. The design rests on one observation: the asynchronous-iteration theorem that
licenses parallel S0 needs exactly the properties (join merges, monotone fires, fixed topology within an epoch) whose
absence P0 found causing defects in today's *sequential* scheduler. Establishing them is therefore the first
deliverable, not a precondition paid for by the second.

In scope: the P0.5 defect batch; the exposure census; merge and fire laws for S0; DIRECT S0 at K = 1 inside BSP's
stratum loop; K > 1 with ownership, lanes and quiescence detection; the observation contract (O-3); retirement of the
scaffolding this replaces.

Out of scope: H-F's flat storage tier and region-based forks until BSP-LE 3 supplies the workload (§6.7; the D.1
design §6 stays the starting point); interning structured lattice values (Stage 2 Q3, a later track); a partitioner;
distributed execution; the visual debugger's UI and error explanation on rounds (G-3; DEFERRED 176 (c), (d)).

## 2. Value — restated

1. **Correctness, independent of speed.** P0 found order-dependence that BSP has *today*: lost updates on undeclared
   writes and through the worldview's replacement merge (DEFERRED 157), dropped flags on in-fire installs (158), a
   type merge that can erase a contradiction (F3(b)), and reverse-order divergences in 249 of the suite's 12 527
   multi-result rounds (P0 §11.4). P0.5 and P1 remove these classes structurally.
2. **Sequential speed at K = 1** (P2): removing the round protocol (snapshot, `fire-result`, diff, `bulk-merge-writes`).
   MEASUREMENT-based models: 1.49× (constraints-adversarial) and 1.35× (type-adversarial) per file; corpus-wide
   1.08–1.11× (P0 §10.2). P2 measures the real number.
3. **Parallel speed at K > 1** (P3). Models: H-S + K=8 gives 4.07× / 3.26× on the heavy files and 1.26–1.34× on the
   corpus. ⚠ **New in D.2, CONJECTURE**: those models assume every S0 fire can run in parallel. If the type cells stay
   fenced until PPN 4C supplies a join for them (§6.3, O-12), the realizable K>1 share before then is the S0 work *not*
   writing type cells, and P0 never measured that share. **P0-8 measures it** (§10.1), and P3's entry gate uses it.
4. **The search payoff** waits on BSP-LE 3 putting search on the network (P0 §2.4). PAR 4 supplies the substrate and
   the contract (§11).

## 5. The design on one page

**Three results carry it.**
- *Asynchronous iteration* — THEOREM (Üresin–Dubois 1990; Bertsekas 1983; Cousot RR-88). Hypotheses **H**: a monotone,
  inflationary operator on a product of finite-height lattices; each component has one writer whose view of it is never
  stale; reads are causal; the schedule is fair; staleness is finite; the topology is fixed during the iteration. Then
  every fair schedule reaches the same least fixpoint.
- *CALM* — THEOREM (Ameloot, Neven, Van den Bussche 2013). Monotone ⇔ coordination-free; fixpoint *detection* is the
  non-monotone part. Hence one coordination per stratum transition.
- *Kleene iteration* — THEOREM (Cousot & Cousot 1977), under H and propagator statelessness
  (`DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.org:700`): **BSP's rounds are the Kleene iterates** xₙ₊₁ = x⁰ ⊔ F(xₙ) (D.1 A.8 item 2). BSP is
  therefore the *reference dynamics*: a replay oracle, and the canonical trace for observation (§7).

**The design sentence.** *S0 runs under the asynchronous-iteration hypotheses by construction: every S0-written cell
has a join merge (E-checked) or is fenced; every S0 fire is monotone or lives in a declared stratum; no fire changes
topology (G-1). Given that, the scheduler owns K executors; executor i owns the cells assigned to it (round-robin) and
publishes their values directly; a fire runs on the executor that owns its first output, writes its own cells
directly and sends foreign writes as deltas; executors never wait for each other inside S0; quiescence is detected by
a double-counted scan; the stratum boundary is the only barrier, where the sequential non-monotone handlers run as
today. K = 1 is the same code with one executor.*

**Layering** (`DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.org:731`, Orthogonality). Cell: lattice, merge, declared properties (E), storage.
Propagator: reads, writes, monotonicity class (P1b). Scheduler: ownership, executors, detection, observation records.
The fence is a *scheduler* response to a *cell* property, so no cell's meaning depends on which scheduler runs it.

```
         topology / S(−1) / S1 handlers   (sequential, between S0 runs — unchanged in meaning)
                         │ epoch e begins: x⁰ₑ (a pointer at K=1; materialized at K>1)
   ┌─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ S0 epoch e — unfenced fires: DIRECT on K executors                  │
   │   executor i: worklistᵢ · owned cells · lanes[i][*] · prodᵢ consᵢ idle-epochᵢ │
   │   foreign write → lane[owner][me]; owner joins; dependents enqueue   │
   │ fenced fires (E-false outputs): deterministic BSP sub-run, then DIRECT again │
   └─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        detector: Σprod = Σcons ∧ ∀i idle-epochᵢ = e, stable across two scans
        → boundary-epoch := e (late S0 writes tagged ≤ e become 'late-s0-write)
        → epoch record (data) → stratum handlers → epoch e+1 or done
```

**Joins by construction, then E (D2P-2).** P1a adds a small combinator library (`join:flat` with ⊤, `join:max`,
`join:ior`, `join:set`, `join:pointwise`, `join:product`). A merge assembled from them carries a `join` declaration by
construction, and most of the rows above are such assemblies. That is G-2's inductive layer. E samples only the
hand-written merges that remain, so E belongs in the co-inductive column.

**E, made sound.** SRE Track 2H's property machinery exists (`make-sre-domain #:declared-properties`,
`infer-domain-properties`) but is fail-open twice over (note §3): the declared merge is not the installed one, and the
confirming samples cannot fail. P1a fixes both. Properties attach to **the merge installed on the cell** (the
`specialized-cell-meta` `merge-fn` field `propagator.rkt:333`, or the `net-new-cell` merge argument), and the sample
generators must include the arms that fail (unsolved metas, binder types, conflicting bindings), checked for power by
a seeded non-join that must be caught. E is evaluated once per merge function, not per cell, so its cost is a
registration-time cost. Its verdict is a **cell** in SRE's property lattice (`prop-confirmed` / `prop-contradicted`),
not a boolean computed off the network (D2L-4).

**The fence** (option A of P0 §9.6, kept per cell). A cell whose installed merge is E-false is *fenced*: at K = 1
nothing changes (the order is deterministic); at K > 1 every propagator with a fenced output runs in a deterministic
BSP sub-run after the DIRECT run quiesces, and the two alternate until neither has work. Fencing is a scheduler
response to a declared cell property; it is never decided by reading code: the fence is a monotone map from a merge's
property cell to {fenced, free} (contradicted ⇒ fenced). A fenced cell has no owner during the DIRECT phase; it is
written only in the sub-run, on the scheduler thread with the executors parked (D2L-2). The fenced set is reported in
every epoch record (D2P-4) and is the fence's **exit criterion**: it retires cell by cell, and its owner for the type
cells is O-12 (D2P-1).

*The simpler alternative (D2P-5, kept open)*: a network with any fenced cell runs at K = 1. It is trivially correct and
needs no alternation, but elaboration networks nearly all contain type cells, so it would leave them sequential until
PPN delivers. **Decision rule**: if P0-8 measures a small S0 share outside type-cell writers, take the whole-network
rule; otherwise the alternation.

### 6.5 Fire context (decided in D.2; open to critique)

Three parameters are bound around every fire today: `current-bsp-fire-round?` and `current-cell-id-namespace`
(`parameterize` at `propagator.rkt:2884`), and `current-source-loc` (in `fire-propagator` `:377`). Together they cost
≈ 496 B of a light fire's 928 B, and an executor thread would inherit its creator's values (P0 §9.5).
- `current-bsp-fire-round?` (11 production reads, each read; D2R-1). 9 choose between a topology *request* and an

- `current-cell-id-namespace` (4 reads, in the `net-new-cell` variants `propagator.rkt:1326`, `:1371`, `:1452`,

unwrapped fires (row 1: fires compute on labelled values instead of filtering by a worldview, D2L-1), the `set-box!` on `current-prop-net-box` in the retry bridge (row 9), and

### 6.6 P2 — DIRECT S0 at K = 1, inside BSP's stratum loop

P0 §4.4 established that the sequential drain (`run-to-quiescence-inner`) is not the DIRECT-K=1 oracle: it has no
stratum layer and no fire-once semantics. P2 builds the S0 inner loop that replaces BSP's (`inner-loop`

### 6.7 P3 — K > 1

- **Executors**: persistent OS-level threads (Racket CS parallel threads, `#:pool 'own`), created once per process,
  parked between solves; one semaphore wake per solve (7.3 µs, paid once), spin with bounded backoff inside a solve
  (≈ 60 ns hops). K is `scheduler-k`, a cell on the scheduler's network (O-4), default `max(1, physical cores − 2)`,
  never oversubscribed (contention analysis §4).
- **Owned records**: at K > 1 a cell's live value is a slot owned by one executor, published by a single pointer store
  under release and read under acquire; the CHAMP value form is **materialized at boundaries** for stratum handlers,
  forks, `.pnet` and observation, and never maintained during S0 (that maintenance *is* the protocol cost H-S removes).
  ⚠ The memory-model claim needs Racket CS's documented guarantees for unsafe/atomic stores across parallel threads;
  D.1 Observation 4 owed the citation and D.2 still owes it (risk R-2).
- **Ownership**: round-robin (or block-64) by cell id; a fire's home is the owner of its first declared output, a
  **derived read** from the assignment, not a field: the `propagator` struct (`propagator.rkt:368`, 7 production
  constructor sites) and `.pnet` do not change (D2R-2). Measured adequate: ≤ 10 % foreign writes (P0 Q4). Fenced
  cells have no owner during the DIRECT phase (D2L-2).
- **Lanes**: `lane[owner][writer]`, single-producer single-consumer, append-only; the owner drains its K−1 lanes at
  the top of each loop iteration and joins. Duplicates are harmless only because P1 made the merges joins.
- **Detection**: per executor, padded: `prod`, `cons`, `idle-epoch`. The detector declares quiescence of epoch e when
  Σprod = Σcons and every idle-epoch = e, stable across two scans (Mattern's double count). Measured: 198–485 ns per
  message on a dependency chain at K = 4–16, against 687–5 858 for rounds (P0 §4.1).
- **Late writes**: `boundary-epoch` is a max-counter; a write carrying epoch ≤ boundary-epoch is a classified
  contradiction, `'late-s0-write`. This replaces D.1's frozen bit and its reset (§9).
- **The fence** at K > 1, as §6.3.
- **Contradiction**: ⊤ in the contradiction cell; executors see it at their next lane check and stop. Monotone.
- **Gate**: the parity suite at K ∈ {1, 4, 8, 16} × 2 seeds; the full suite at K = 8; the replay monitor at K > 1; the
  per-fire allocation rate at K > 1 (A-12, owed since P0 §7); detection latency ≤ the scan row × 1.5; and the
  realized speedup reported against the §2 item 3 models *including* the fenced share.

## 7. Observation contract (O-3, decided 2026-10-15)

Analysis: D.1 Appendix A.6–A.13. In short:
- **Records are data, delivered at boundaries**, keyed by one shared key space **(network, epoch, round)**. Forks are
  their own network ids. No consumer code runs inside the scheduler.
- **Epoch records** come from every scheduler, including Tier-1 flushes and the boundary's own step: x⁰ₑ → x*ₑ on the
  changed cells, the set of propagators fired, and the epoch's fenced cells (D2P-4). They depend on the program only,
  under H. The changed set is not a diff walk: each executor adds a cell to its own changed-set when an owned cell
  strictly increases, and the epoch's set is their union at the boundary (D2M-2).
- **Round records** are the Kleene iterates, on request. Under BSP they are the run; under DIRECT they come from the
  replay. Each carries (cell, old value, held value, writers, round), replacing `source-propagator` (DEFERRED 176 (a)).
  With these, a cell's value unfolds into a derivation in which every premise sits at an earlier round, which is
  well-founded on cycles (D.1 A.13.1, measured).
- **Telemetry** is a separate stream per scheduler (BSP keeps `current-bsp-round-stats`; DIRECT gets per-executor
  counters). The execution view is reserved and is built only if P3's debugging needs executor order.
- **Placement**: round records in P1 or P2; epoch records, keys, the replay and telemetry separation in P2; DIRECT
  telemetry in P3. Outside PAR 4: DEFERRED 176 (c), (d).
- **O-11** (how an observation request is scoped) is posed in §15.

## 9. NTT speculative model (revised)

```prologos
;; ── lattices ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
type Epoch := Nat
impl Lattice Nat :as max                         ; K, epochs, prod/cons counters
  join | a b -> [max a b]
  bot -> 0

type Assignment := Map Epoch [Map CellId ExecutorIdx]   ; ownership per topology epoch
impl Lattice Assignment :kind :value
  join | a b -> [map-join-flat a b]               ; per key: equal ⇒ same, different ⇒ ⊤ (D2L-3); each epoch's
                                                  ; entry is written once, at that epoch's topology run;
                                                  ; the current ownership is a derived read

type Lane := Set [CellId Delta]                  ; deltas are lattice elements; a set because merges are joins
impl Lattice Lane
  join | a b -> [set-union a b]

type Worldview := [Bits committed] [Bits invalidated]   ; P1a row 1
impl Lattice Worldview :kind :value
  join | [c1 i1] [c2 i2] -> [[ior c1 c2] [ior i1 i2]]
;; derived read (not stored): live = committed ∧ ¬invalidated

type EpochRecord := Map [Network Epoch] [Changed Fired Fenced]  ; written once per (network, epoch)
impl Lattice EpochRecord
  join | a b -> [map-join-flat a b]               ; ⊤ on a second, different write (D2L-3)

type MergeProps := PropLattice                   ; SRE's prop-confirmed / prop-contradicted, per merge (D2L-4)
;; derived, monotone: fence(c) = [contradicted? (props (merge c))]

;; ── scheduler-state cells (ArenaSchedulerNet, lifetime :persistent, O-4) ──
cell scheduler-k     : Cell Nat
cell epoch           : Cell Epoch                ; current S0 epoch (max)
cell boundary-epoch  : Cell Epoch                ; last epoch declared quiescent (max)
cell assignment      : Cell Assignment
cell exec-prod       : Cell (Vec K Nat)          ; padded in the realization
cell exec-cons       : Cell (Vec K Nat)
cell idle-epoch      : Cell (Vec K Epoch)        ; replaces D.1's reset flag
cell lanes           : Cell (Vec K (Vec K Lane))
cell exec-changed    : Cell (Vec K (Set CellId))  ; owned cells that strictly increased this epoch (D2M-2)
cell epoch-records   : Cell EpochRecord
cell merge-props     : Cell (Map MergeId MergeProps)   ; E's verdicts and the combinators' declarations

;; ── propagators ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
propagator s0-fire [p : PropagatorId]
  :reads  [Cell L ...]                ; p's declared inputs, any owner
  :writes [Cell L ...]                ; own outputs directly; foreign outputs via lanes
  :requires [monotone p] [∀ o ∈ outputs p. join? (merge o) ∨ fenced o]   ; P1a, P1b
  [fire p]

propagator owner-drain [i : ExecutorIdx]
  :reads  [lanes[i][*]]
  :writes [owned cells of i]
  [for-each-delta [c d] [join-into c d]]

propagator quiescence-detector
  :reads  [exec-prod exec-cons idle-epoch epoch]
  :writes [boundary-epoch]
  :non-monotone                        ; the one non-monotone act: "nothing more will arrive" (CALM)
  [when [and [= [sum prod] [sum cons]] [all-equal idle-epoch epoch] stable-twice] [epoch]]

propagator epoch-recorder
  :reads  [boundary-epoch]
  :writes [epoch-records]
  [record [network epoch] changed fired]

;; ── stratification ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
stratification arena-scheduler
  :fiber S0        [s0-fire owner-drain]              ; monotone; K executors; no barrier
  :fiber S0-fenced [s0-fire on fenced outputs]        ; monotone fires, non-join merges; deterministic BSP sub-run
  :fiber boundary  [quiescence-detector epoch-recorder]
  :fiber topology  [assigner topology-requests]       ; writes the next epoch's assignment entry
  :fiber S(-1)     [process-retraction]
  :fiber S1        [naf-handler]
  :order S0 <-> S0-fenced -> boundary -> topology -> S(-1) -> S1 -> S0
```

**Observations.**
1. *On-network?* Every scheduler cell is now monotone. D.1's two resets became comparisons of max-counters
   (`idle-epoch` against `epoch`; `boundary-epoch` against a write's epoch), and the LWW partition became an
   epoch-indexed, write-once assignment whose current value is a derived read. **The only non-monotone act left is the
   detector's predicate**, which CALM says cannot be otherwise. Off-network, as in D.1: the threads and padded vectors
   that realize these cells.
2. *Impurities revealed.* (a) `S0 <-> S0-fenced` is an alternation inside one stratum. It is sound only because both
   fibers' fires are monotone; the fenced merges are what make the combined result order-dependent, and that is
   exactly what the fence contains. A theorem for the alternation's result would need the fenced merges to be at least
   inflationary, which F3(b) is not. CONJECTURE that the clash ⇒ ⊤ half makes `type-unify-or-top` inflationary; P1a
   tests it with E's samples. (b) `:requires` on `s0-fire` is a *static* obligation that NTT cannot state today.
3. *NTT gaps.* No form for a merge's declared properties (`:join`), a propagator's monotonicity class, the fence, or
   placement. These are the same metadata `specialized-cell-meta` carries; candidates for the NTT design.
4. *Inexpressible.* The memory model (release/acquire), as in D.1.

  *Three grades of determinism*. The names are descriptive; the distinctions are facts.
  - **Wall clock** varies from run to run: CV ≈ 0.05 on identical runs (W2-10-7).
  - **Deterministic for one implementation and one input order.** Precedents:
    - Z3's `rlimit`: "as long as you keep using the same binary, it is deterministic" (C. Wintersteiger, Z3
      developer, Stack Overflow q. 45457131).
    - Dafny measures verification cost in resource units for this reason (dafny.org, *Verification Optimization*).
    - Lean's heartbeats give "'deterministic' timeouts" (Lean reference, *IO / Timing*), yet differ between GMP and
      non-GMP builds.
    - SWI-Prolog's depth limit "may differ from the depth computed based on a theoretical model"
      (`call_with_depth_limit/3`).
    - Our own fire counts belong here too.
  - **A function of the program**: invariant across schedulers, install order, K, and runtimes yet to be written.
    These systems stop at the second grade because a search has no semantic notion of a step. **Our computation has
    one: the Kleene step.** That is already the logical clock of O-3 (§7: round records are the Kleene iterates; under
    DIRECT they come from the replay).

  *Options*:
  - (a) **A contract in logical time**, in four parts.
    1. **Semantic budgets count Kleene steps.**
       - The network budget bounds the depth of the Kleene iteration per quiescence run; this amends O-2.
       - BSP counts the rounds that changed state.
       - DIRECT carries a depth label per cell, and certifies "within budget" when every label ≤ B (THEOREM below).
       - When a label would pass B, DIRECT stops, and BSP replays B rounds from the epoch's start state to decide
         exactly. The replay is the oracle §7 already keeps.
       - Fenced cells have no Kleene depth (their merge is not a join), so they run under BSP and count its rounds.
       - Precedent in our own tree: widening already bounds by rounds (`propagator.rkt:4295`).
    2. **Resource guards are separate, and a guard's trip is never an answer.**
       - A fire guard backstops DIRECT, and lattices with an infinite ascending chain below x_B. `:timeout` becomes
         the documented wall-clock deadline.
       - A trip is a classified, per-command outcome that names the guard, its value, and the key that raises it.
       - Precedents: SMT's `unknown`; SWI-Prolog's `inference_limit_exceeded`, distinct from failure; Lean's
         "(deterministic) timeout … maximum number of heartbeats (N) has been reached (use 'set_option maxHeartbeats
         <num>' …)".
    3. **Every exhaustion is a value on one channel, per command**: DEFERRED 172 and 153, 182's abort, `u9`'s
       misreport, and W2-10-2's unread contradictions. An exhaustion inside an ATMS branch is not a nogood.
    4. **The surface does what it says.**
       - An override is a record update: it changes the keys it names and nothing else. That is 182's fix shape.
       - A key that is validated but never read (`:parallelism`, `:fairness`, `:scheduler-io`;
         `macros.rkt:1017-1028`) is rejected at the definition as reserved and not implemented, or removed.
  - (b) **D.2 as written, plus the defect fixes.** Fuel counts changes and is budgeted at Σ heights (§6.6, §8). On
    finite-height networks nothing exhausts under any schedule (THEOREM, P0 §5); below that bound the verdict depends
    on the schedule. Parts 3 and 4 of (a) are kept. `:timeout` is documented as the fire budget it is, or renamed.
  - (c) **Today's units; fix only the defects** (172, 153, 182, `u9`, W2-10-2). The unit is revisited when P2 forces
    it.

  *Lean (a)*. The reasons:
  1. **Orthogonality.** `DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.org:785-787` names "cell semantics depending on scheduler-specific
     execution mode" as the anti-pattern that "violates CALM's order-independence". `:794-796` asks that every
     scheduler run "the same network identically".
     - The fuel cell is scheduler state (`:905`), but its threshold writes the contradiction cell, a semantic outcome.
     - Under fires or changes, that outcome depends on the scheduler at the margin (fact 2).
     - Under (a), the one semantic verdict is a function of the program. The scheduler's own counts become guards,
       and their trips are labelled as operational.
  2. **Correct by construction; illegal states unrepresentable** (`:13-17`, `:327-354`).
     - Today a budget trip can surface as any of five things: a full answer set, fewer answers, a protocol violation,
       a silent fallback, or a lost file (fact 1).
     - In a logic language, "exhausted" read as "false" is a **wrong answer**, not a slow one.
     - SWI-Prolog and SMT solvers keep three outcomes distinct: proved, refuted, and not decided within the budget.
  3. **Coherence with O-3** (decided 2026-10-15). "Is the fixpoint reached by step B, and what is x_B?" is an

     (network, epoch, round), with no new mechanism.
  4. **Decomplection** (`:39-43`). Fires and changes braid together *large* and *divergent*: 64 independent copies
     cost 64 fires at depth 1.
     - A work budget is a cap on program size. A depth budget detects divergence, and its scale follows the span, not
       the width. Span is the axis PAR 4B's Stage 1 measures.
     - Under G-4's distributed, incremental compilation, networks grow. A fixed fire budget would eventually reject
       correct large programs.
  5. **Progressive Disclosure** (`:25-30`). A newcomer never meets `'tropical-fuel-exhausted` (W2-10-2). They meet
     "not decided within B steps", and B is the same on every machine and every run.

  **THEOREM (the depth certificate).**
  - *Assumptions*:
    - (H1) every merge is a join;
    - (H2) every fire is monotone and reads only its declared inputs (W2-9's E1);
    - (H3) the run starts at x₀, with every label 0;
    - (H4) when p strictly changes c, label(c) := max(label(c), 1 + max over p's inputs of label(input)).
  - *Claim*: at every point, val(c) ⊑ x_{label(c)}(c).
  - *Proof*: by induction on writes. p writes f_p(val(in)) ⊑ f_p(x_m) ⊑ x_{m+1}(c), by monotonicity and the Kleene
    step, and the iterates increase.
  - *Consequence*: at quiescence val = lfp (chaotic iteration; Cousot 1977). So with L the largest label, x_L = lfp,
    and the depth is at most L. Each change raises the largest label by at most 1, so depth ≤ L ≤ changes: the change
    count is a free certificate, only looser.
  - *Checked* (MEASUREMENT): 0 violations in 12 converging runs, and a mutant without the +1 is caught (label 0
    against depth 8).
  - *Not tight*: L exceeds the depth on the union cycle (6 vs 4) and the climb (9 vs 8). The replay closes the gap
    exactly, and only on the exhaustion path.

  *Counter-case* (the strongest):
  - **Cost.** (a) puts a label read per input and a label write per change on DIRECT's hot path. That cost is
    unmeasured.
  - **The margin may never be visited.** The default never binds a correct program (10 rounds against 1 000 000
    fires; fact 3), so users who do not set budgets may never reach the window where the verdict depends on the
    schedule.
    - The answer to that: logic programmers *do* set budgets to bound search, and that is exactly where the window
      and the partial state show.
  - **Divergence costs twice.** A divergent run pays the DIRECT attempt plus B BSP rounds before it reports.
  - **A fire count stays anyway**, as a guard: DIRECT's termination under suppression needs the ascending chain
    condition below x_B.
  - **Part 4 breaks an acceptance file.** `examples/2026-03-18-track7-acceptance.prologos:161-168` declares
    `:fairness :priority` and `:scheduler-io :blocking-ok`.
  - **(c)'s case**: decide the unit when DIRECT exists to measure it.

  *What settles it*:
  1. **P2's gate measures the labels' cost** on the heavy files. If it exceeds the A/B's noise (CV ≈ 0.05), certify
     with the change count instead, and replay more often.
  2. **The depth of every S0 run on the standard library** sizes the default. PAR 4B's Stage-1 profile measures the
     same quantity, as span per S0 run.
  3. **An exhaustion inside an ATMS branch** needs its own outcome rather than a nogood. That meets 177's premise sets
     (SC-30), so it is coordinated with O-17.

  *Placement, if (a)*:
  - PAR 4 does part 1 in P2, which rewrites the fuel cell's on-write check anyway (§6.6), and writes the contract

  The condition costs no semantics. The certificate only decides how often the exact replay runs; it never decides

    - By the THEOREM above, depth ≤ L ≤ changes.
