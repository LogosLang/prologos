### D4.P3a item 18 — a dynamic key may hit any label, so every field's type widens (`b9002e46`)

- **Why**: D16's dyn-key `map-assoc` kept every known field's TYPE verbatim and flipped only the tail, so `{:host String …}` survived an assoc of `42` — and a select laundered that into a CLOSED `{:host String}` over an Int. - **Shape**: `record-widen-all-with` is the TYPE-side dual of D24's `record-mark-all-unknown` — dissoc makes PRESENCE uncertain and leaves the type a fact; assoc makes the TYPE uncertain and leaves presence a fact. Tighter than the D24 sibling's fresh meta, because assoc knows V.
