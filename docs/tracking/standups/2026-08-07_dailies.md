### Slice 7 — a paren goal keeps its implicit solve under a postfix access (`f54dfc6c`)

**Why.** `(fruit-color "apple" c)` ran the query; `(fruit-color "apple" c):c`

**Surprise 1 — the obvious fix is UNSOUND, and only a measurement shows it.**
