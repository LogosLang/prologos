Options space includes: (i) default-to-error at freeze/default-metas (in-function precedent exists: three per-domain policies) — but freeze fires in NON-display contexts (stored types, constraint rendering, capture): needs a context split or provenance gate; (ii) an end-of-command sweep -> error on provenance-tagged unsolved metas (the check-unresolved-trait-constraints precedent) — but D19 mint sites pass BARE STRINGS (no srcloc; needs structured meta-source upgrade); (iii) keep permissive display, tighten ONLY the refusal legs (e.g. solve V := ⋃knowns ∪ fresh from dyn rows instead of refusing); (iv) annotation-derived bounds — NO machinery exists (meta-info.constraints is dead-threaded; flag as F-row/carrier-change-era?). Also: fix the SHALLOW unsolved-metas-to-holes walk (no expr-union/expr-Record recursion — D19 metas survive into stored types) regardless of posture?
